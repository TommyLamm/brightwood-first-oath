# Sand Dune Pass environment prompts

All three images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-24. No reference images were passed to ImageGen; existing shore layers and the environment manifest were inspected for project conventions. The generated source PNGs are preserved alongside this file.

## Sky

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: opaque sky background layer for a side-scrolling fantasy game, later exported to 960x540
Primary request: Create an original sky backdrop for Sand Dune Pass, a traversable sandstone canyon in Brightwood First Oath.
Scene/backdrop: A wide open evening sky above a desert pass, with soft blue distant haze low on the horizon and a gentle golden glow; keep the central playfield visually calm and readable.
Style/medium: Charming crisp pixel-art fantasy game background, deliberate pixel clusters, clean dark-brown outlines where silhouettes meet the sky; polished and friendly, matching a warm amber-and-sandstone adventure palette.
Composition/framing: Landscape 16:9, full-frame sky only; upper sky mostly clear, thin warm clouds near the distant horizon; no land or ground in the lower edge.
Lighting/mood: Golden evening light, calm adventurous mood.
Color palette: Deep clear blue at the top easing to lighter blue and warm amber close to the horizon.
Constraints: Fully opaque RGB sky layer. Original composition. No terrain, dunes, canyon walls, arches, objects, caravans, characters, creatures, enemies, interface, text, logos, map labels, frame, or watermark.
Avoid: Photorealism, gradients that obscure gameplay, detailed cloud cover in the center, extra decorative marks.
```

## Far

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent far-parallax background layer for a side-scrolling fantasy game, later exported to 960x540
Primary request: Create the distant scenery for Sand Dune Pass, a traversable canyon in Brightwood First Oath.
Scene/backdrop: Layered wind-carved sandstone arches, distant mesas and narrow canyon ridges, with blue atmospheric haze. Suggest a quiet old caravan route using only a couple of tiny, half-buried wagon-wheel or marker-stone relic silhouettes far in the distance, kept small and away from the central route.
Style/medium: Charming crisp pixel-art fantasy game background, deliberate readable pixel clusters, dark brown outlines and warm amber sandstone highlights, matching a friendly adventure game.
Composition/framing: Wide landscape 16:9 transparent layer. Place the main distant arches and ridges low in frame, mostly in the bottom 40 percent; stagger several depth bands. Keep the center open and readable with a clear traversable canyon corridor. Leave the upper 60 percent and open spaces between arches truly transparent.
Lighting/mood: Soft golden evening light with cool blue distance haze.
Color palette: Amber, sandstone, ochre and muted russet, fading into desaturated blue haze in the far distance.
Materials/textures: Wind-cut sandstone strata and smooth rounded arch edges; small quiet relics partially buried in distant sand.
Constraints: True transparent alpha PNG; only the far scenery silhouettes are opaque. No sky fill or clouds, no solid black background, no continuous ground plane, no lower foreground obstruction. No characters, enemies, combat, interface, text, logos, map labels, frame or watermark.
Avoid: Dense detail in the center, photorealism, exaggerated outlines, repeating tiled pattern.
```

## Mid

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent middle-parallax environment layer for a side-scrolling fantasy game, later exported to 960x540
Primary request: Create the nearer framing scenery for Sand Dune Pass, complementing the separate sky and distant canyon layer.
Scene/backdrop: Close wind-carved sandstone walls and partial arch edges frame a traversable canyon. Use only a few sparse tufts of hardy desert grass near the outer corners. The pass remains open and walkable through the center.
Style/medium: Charming crisp pixel-art fantasy game environment, deliberate readable pixel clusters, dark brown outlines, warm amber highlights and clear sandstone strata; friendly polished adventure-game art.
Composition/framing: Wide landscape 16:9 transparent PNG. Put closer canyon wall fragments and arch edges along the far left and far right margins only, rising up the sides and tapering away toward the upper outer thirds. Keep the central 65 percent completely open and transparent from top to bottom; keep the full lower 15 percent of the canvas transparent across the entire width so gameplay ground stays clear. No object crosses into the lower central route.
Lighting/mood: Golden evening rim light with cool blue haze behind the opening.
Color palette: Golden amber, pale sandstone, ochre and muted russet, with restrained blue haze visible only through openings.
Materials/textures: Layered eroded sandstone and rounded wind-carved edges; crisp small pixel clusters, no soft painted gradients.
Constraints: True transparent alpha PNG; only side-framing rock fragments and sparse outer-corner scrub are opaque. No sky fill, no black background, no continuous ground plane. No characters, enemies, interface, text, logos, map labels, frame or watermark.
Avoid: Blocking the center, details on the lower ground line, close foreground rocks across the route, large relics, photorealism, repeating tile pattern.
```
