## Integration delivered

- `src/main.ts` creates the single Phaser game, app scenes, app UI, Playroom CloudPort, and guarded browser localStorage adapter. `src/app/scenes.ts` owns map load-before-transition completion and reloads the renderer when the restored world revision changes.
- `src/app/app.ts` initializes persistence and required manifests concurrently, holds startup incomplete when asset validation fails, supports continue, two-step new-character replacement, temporary play, load/save retry, cloud conflict handling, settings checkpoints, and save-change fingerprinting. Returning to title preserves the active persistent session so queued saves are not cancelled; returning from temporary play reloads the persistent save. It does not checkpoint when persistence initialization failed or while temporary play is active.
- `src/app/gameplay.ts` rebuilds runtime-only actors from SaveData and repairs an arena save to `mine-gate/right`; runtime enemies, drops, attacks, telegraphs, effects, and guard state are reconstructed or cleared rather than persisted. Successful map transitions checkpoint after the renderer load; a failed map load preserves the current location and progress.
- `src/app/root-ui.ts` connects title/create flows, status and retry controls, combat, panels, inventory/equipment/skills/stats/quests/map/shop/settings, and lifecycle actions to app commands. Startup asset errors do not offer save retry controls that cannot succeed until assets load.
- `src/app/state.ts` and `src/app/scenes.ts` carry a world revision so Continue, cloud reload, and same-map restored sessions refresh renderer state.
