## App and gameplay integration

- `src/app/app.ts`, `state.ts`, `command-queue.ts`, and `fixed-step.ts` own app state, sequenced commands, fixed-step time, RNG and entity IDs, and focus/pause behavior.
- `src/app/assets.ts` validates the four canonical manifest shapes, required keys, safe relative URLs, uniqueness, and animation references. Missing or invalid packs produce explicit startup diagnostics; empty-pack fallback is not used.
- `src/app/catalog.ts`, `keyboard.ts`, `panels.ts`, `scenes.ts`, and `root-ui.ts` compose content, handle rebinding and blur release, manage app panels, load renderer maps, and expose UI snapshot/controller helpers.
- `src/app/gameplay.ts` connects movement, combat, damage/invulnerability, once-only monster rewards, drops and pickups, inventory/stat/skill commands, quest acceptance/claims, NPCs, portals, death/respawn, boss results and map transitions.
- This audit aligned NPC and quest interactions to the specified 64px range, required a current nearby merchant interaction before purchases, denied return-to-town during a live arena fight, cleared runtime guard on respawn, and aligned rest recovery with the content spec: rank required, three seconds since attack/damage, then HP-only recovery every two seconds.
- `src/app/index.ts` exports the app API. `src/main.ts` currently creates one Phaser game with Boot/World scenes and the startup/status view; its full UI and persistence wiring remain incomplete.
